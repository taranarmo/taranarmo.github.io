## About Me

I am a **Senior Data & MLOps Engineer** and **Environmental Fluid Dynamics Researcher** based in Berlin. My career spans enterprise data platform architecture, declarative reproducible systems, and high-impact physical oceanography and limnology research.

I bridge two disciplines:
1. **Production Software & Data Engineering**: Building robust, fault-tolerant data platforms, lakehouses, orchestration engines, and low-latency machine learning pipelines across AWS, GCP, and bare-metal environments.
2. **Computational Physical Science**: Numerical modeling of convective turbulence, under-ice hydrodynamics, and thermal stratification with peer-reviewed publications and field acoustic telemetry (ADCP, thermistors).

I believe in **declarative reproducibility**, **strict data contracts**, and **infrastructure-as-code**. As an active open-source contributor, I maintain key data engineering and scientific packages for the **NixOS** ecosystem (`nixpkgs`).
