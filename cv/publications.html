INTERDISCIPLINARY RESEARCH PROJECTS

Lake Pycnoclines Trap Organic Particles Forming Hot Spots of Accelerated Carbon Cycling (PycnoTrap)
Leibniz Institute of Freshwater Ecology and Inland Fisheries (IGB Berlin)
2021 – 2023

Joint limnological project researching sinking velocity, biomass degradation, and hydrodynamic particle trapping across pycnoclines in stratified lake columns. Designed twin-action underwater camera frames and built computer vision / stereo-video tracking pipelines to calculate 3D velocity vectors and estimate vertical particulate carbon flux.

Lake Onego: Life Under the Ice
Northern Water Problems Institute (NWPI) / EPFL Limnology Center · Project Website
2015 – 2017

International multi-institutional field campaigns investigating under-ice convective mixing, thermal boundary regimes, and winter ecosystem dynamics in Europe’s second-largest boreal lake.
